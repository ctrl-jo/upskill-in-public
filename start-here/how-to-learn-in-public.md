# How to learn in public

Learning in public means showing small parts of what you are learning, even before you feel like an expert. It can be a note, a post, or a project. Sharing is optional; private practice counts too.

## A simple routine

1. Study one small topic for a time that fits your day.
2. Write what you learned in your own words using a [daily log](../templates/daily-log.md).
3. If you feel ready, share one small thing: a post, a note, or a project link. A sentence about what worked and what confused you is enough.
4. Choose one next step. There is no need to post every day or compare your pace with anyone else's.

## Post ideas

| Idea | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Daily log | Today I made a button respond to a click. |
| A concept explained simply | A variable is a name for a value my program can use. |
| Mini project | My word counter works for a short sentence; next I will try an empty input. |
| Certificate or milestone | I finished my first module and can explain one new idea. |

## Before sharing

1. Share only your own notes and work. Link to resources instead of copying them, and never upload paid course content.
2. Remove passwords, access keys, personal details, and information about other people.
3. Check any school rules before sharing assignment work. Keep the post private if you are not ready.
