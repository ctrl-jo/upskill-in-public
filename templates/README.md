# Templates

A template is a page you copy and fill in with your own words.

| Template | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [Daily log](daily-log.md) | A short record of one study session. |
| [Study notes](study-notes.md) | Your explanation of a topic, with an example. |
| [Mini project](mini-project.md) | A small project's goal, steps, result, and next improvement. |
| [Progress tracker](progress-tracker.md) | Checkpoints and evidence of what you practiced. |

1. Choose one template from the table.
2. Follow the [GitHub or computer copy steps](../my-learning/README.md) to put it into your own `my-learning/` folder.
3. Rename it for its purpose, such as `2026-10-16-daily-log.md`, using your own date. Use `progress-tracker.md` for a tracker you update over time.
4. Replace bracketed fields with your words and save the file. An unfinished note is fine.
