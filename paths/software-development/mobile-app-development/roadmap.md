# Mobile app development roadmap

## Coming soon

The full guided route and examples are Planned. This original high-level outline is a preview.

1. Choose one platform: Android or iOS (Apple's phone operating system). Read its beginner setup requirements in [resources](resources.md) before installing tools.
2. Learn the platform's main language: Kotlin for Android or Swift for iOS. Practice named values, choices, repeated actions, and reusable instructions.
3. Follow one official beginner lesson to show text and a button on a screen. Check that pressing the button changes something.
4. Build a two-screen study checklist and practice moving between the screens.
5. Save a small list on the device, then check whether it remains after closing and reopening the app.
6. Try the app on an available device or an emulator (a program that imitates a device). Write down one problem and fix it.
7. Describe the app and share your own notes or a short demonstration. Publishing to an app store is optional and may involve fees.
