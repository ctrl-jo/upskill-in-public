# Microsoft Azure beginner roadmap

This original route introduces Azure and leads into AZ-900, an optional fundamentals exam. It is a starting study guide, not a replacement for the current official exam outline. Reading and written projects require no paid cloud account.

1. Read the [cloud introduction](../intro/README.md). For your first 10 minutes, write one sentence each about compute, storage, and networking in your daily log.
2. Choose a beginner Microsoft Learn module from [resources](resources.md). Explain the difference between renting computing tools and managing your own computers.
3. Learn service models: infrastructure as a service (renting basic computing resources), platform as a service (using a managed place to run an app), and software as a service (using a finished application). Give one possible use for each.
4. Learn Azure's organization: a region is a geographic area, a resource is a service you create, a resource group organizes related resources, and a subscription groups access and billing. Draw how a small study app could be organized.
5. Compare ways to run programs, store files, and connect services. Match one compute choice and one storage choice to your study app. Describe your choices in your own words.
6. Learn how people sign in and receive permission to use resources. Explain why a student app should grant only the access it needs.
7. Explore cost estimates, rules for managing resources, and monitoring (checking a service's health). Write a plan to estimate costs and remove practice services after use. Budget alerts are notices, not a guaranteed spending stop.
8. Read the current [official AZ-900 study guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/az-900) and compare its topics with your notes. Use the free practice material it links to and revisit ideas you cannot yet explain. Booking the paid exam is optional.
9. Make a small written project: a diagram and explanation of your study app's services, permissions, and cost plan. Save it in [my-learning](../../../my-learning/README.md) and share your own explanation when ready.
